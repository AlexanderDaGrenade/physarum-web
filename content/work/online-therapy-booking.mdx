## The problem

People needing mental-health support face long waits and awkward booking. Privacy has to come first.

## What we built

- A full-stack booking platform: choose a licensed psychologist, pick a time slot, pay securely.
- Private online sessions with a user-friendly flow from first visit to appointment.
- An AI chatbot and the complete UI/UX design.

## The result

**Accessible, private therapy that patients can book in minutes.**
