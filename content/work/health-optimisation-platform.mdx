## The problem

Blood test results are hard to interpret, and improving them usually means repeated doctor visits.

## What we built

- A full-stack platform with a free course on optimising bloodwork.
- AI-assisted bloodwork analysis with personalised, tailored guidance.
- The complete UI/UX design.

## The result

**A platform that helps people understand and improve more than 60 key biomarkers within 90 days — without a doctor’s visit.**
