## The problem

Most assistants forget you between conversations. The client wanted one that learns who you are and adapts.

## What we built

- A complete cross-platform mobile app in React Native.
- Mood and emotion recognition, plus memory of preferences and habits.
- A trained chatbot, device integration and a privacy-first design.

## The result

**A personal assistant that gives intelligent, empathetic and secure support — proactively, not just on request.**
