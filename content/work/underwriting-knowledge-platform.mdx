## The problem

Insurance agencies were calling underwriters for answers that already existed somewhere — and getting them wrong led to unnecessary cancellations.

## What we built

- A full-stack platform, frontend to backend, holding a continually updated base of underwriting resources.
- An AI chatbot developed and trained on that knowledge so agents get answers instantly.
- The complete UI/UX design.

## The result

**A simple, affordable knowledge base agencies can rely on — fewer calls to underwriting, fewer cancellations, and faster answers.**
