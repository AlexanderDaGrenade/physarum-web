## The problem

Veterinary students and professionals needed one place for anatomy, health conditions and treatments — and flat diagrams weren’t enough.

## What we built

- Interactive 3D animal anatomy models for in-depth learning.
- A health dictionary covering conditions, treatments and supplements for animals and humans.
- An AI chatbot, dark mode and the complete UI/UX design.

## The result

**An educational platform that makes animal anatomy and health information easy to explore.**
