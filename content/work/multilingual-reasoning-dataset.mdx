## The problem

A foundation-model team needed reasoning data in several languages, written by people who actually know the subject — not translated, not templated.

## The path

- Recruited and vetted domain experts per language, with a calibration round before production.
- Wrote multi-step solutions with explicit reasoning, then had a second expert review every item.
- Tracked inter-annotator agreement per batch and fed disagreements back into the guidelines.
