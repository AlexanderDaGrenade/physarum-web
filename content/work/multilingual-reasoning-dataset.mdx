## The problem

A model-training team needed fine-tuning data that would teach real reasoning — not templated answers, and not data that quietly contradicts itself.

## The path

- Wrote prompts and expert responses with explicit, step-by-step reasoning, following detailed project guidelines.
- Ran every item through multi-stage review: a second expert checks correctness, completeness and instruction-following.
- Fed recurring review findings back into the guidelines so the same mistake didn't happen twice.

## The result

**Training data delivered with a review trail for every item.** Specific volumes and quality metrics are available to prospective clients under NDA.
