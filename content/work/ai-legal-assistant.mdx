## The problem

Legal advice is expensive and hard to understand. The client wanted an assistant that gives ordinary people clear answers to complex legal questions.

## What we built

- A full-stack web application, from the database to the interface.
- An AI chatbot developed and trained to turn legal questions into concise, actionable guidance.
- A UI/UX design that keeps a complex subject approachable.

## The result

**A live AI legal assistant that makes guidance accessible to everyone.**
