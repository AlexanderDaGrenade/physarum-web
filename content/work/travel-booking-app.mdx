## The problem

Travellers juggle separate sites for flights and cruises. The client wanted one simple app for both.

## What we built

- A complete mobile app for comparing prices, viewing schedules and saving wishlists.
- Booking for both flights and cruises in a single streamlined flow.
- An in-app chatbot and a privacy-focused design.

## The result

**One app to plan and book trips by air and sea.**
