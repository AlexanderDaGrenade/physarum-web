## The problem

Publishers wanted visitors to stay longer and engage more, without writing extra content.

## What we built

- AI-powered content analysis that reads a website’s pages.
- Real-time generation of contextual quizzes, with multilingual support.
- An embeddable widget and the full product UI/UX.

## The result

**A SaaS that turns existing content into interactive quizzes — more time on site and new advertising inventory.**
